## Definition

Fairness in AI concerns whether the design, operation, and outcomes of an AI system treat individuals and groups in ways that are ethically justified, non-discriminatory, and appropriate to the decision context.

## Core Idea

Fairness is a normative concept, not merely a statistical property.

Different mathematical fairness criteria represent different interpretations of what it means to be fair.

A system may satisfy one fairness criterion while violating another.

## Why It Matters

AI systems increasingly allocate:

- opportunities
- resources
- attention
- risks
- benefits
- penalties

Fairness therefore affects both the distribution of outcomes and the legitimacy of AI-supported decisions.

## Measurement / Evaluation

Fairness can be evaluated through:

- statistical fairness metrics
- subgroup performance analysis
- procedural analysis
- stakeholder assessment
- legal non-discrimination requirements
- contextual ethical reasoning

Statistical parity alone is not sufficient to establish ethical fairness.
