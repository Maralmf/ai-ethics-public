## Definition

Historical bias occurs when existing social, institutional, or historical inequalities are reflected in the data used by an AI system.

## Core Idea

Even when data collection and model development are technically correct, the data may encode unfair conditions that already exist in society.

## Why It Matters

AI systems trained on historical data may reproduce or amplify past discrimination rather than merely describe reality.

## Mechanism

Historical patterns enter the dataset through past decisions, institutional practices, social inequalities, or unequal access to opportunities.

## Example

A hiring model trained on historical hiring decisions may learn patterns associated with past discrimination if certain groups were systematically underrepresented or disadvantaged.
