> **As a working distinction, AI Ethics provides the normative inquiry and reasoning, while Ethical AI describes an aspiration toward ethically justifiable AI design, use, and governance.**

|Dimension|AI Ethics|Ethical AI|
|---|---|---|
|Nature|Field of normative inquiry|Aspirational characterization|
|Primary question|What ought we do regarding AI?|Can this AI system and its use be ethically justified?|
|Unit of analysis|AI and surrounding socio-technical systems|Particular systems, uses, and governance arrangements|
|Main output|Principles, arguments, obligations, judgments|Evidence-informed claim of ethical acceptability|
|Context dependence|High|High|
|Binary property?|Not applicable|Should not normally be treated as binary|
|Moral agency implied?|No|No|
|Relation to governance|Provides normative foundations|Ethical acceptability is supported, but not guaranteed, by governance|
